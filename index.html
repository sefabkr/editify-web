Editify

The offline PDF editor for iPhone and iPad

Editify puts 21 PDF tools in one app, and every one of them works right on your iPhone or iPad. Edit, sign, scan and convert PDFs without uploading anything. No account. No ads.

Get Editify on the App Store.

New in 1.2

  • Ask the Document: summarize a PDF and ask it questions with Apple Intelligence, entirely on device. Every answer shows the page it comes from. Free on devices that support Apple Intelligence.
  • A new Home: pick a document and see it after each tool, with what will change, such as “42 → 41 pages” or the estimated file size.
  • Spotlight, widgets and Control Center: find your PDFs from Search, and open a tool or start a scan in one tap.
  • iPhone Duo and iPad: full screen on both displays of iPhone Duo, with a two-pane Home on iPad and the open Duo.
  • Certificate signing: sign with your own certificate (.p12/.pfx) as a standard PDF signature.

What's inside

  • Merge, split, extract, delete, rotate, crop and compress PDFs
  • Convert images, Word (DOCX), RTF, TXT, web pages and camera scans to PDF
  • Convert PDF pages to Word (DOCX) as page images that keep their appearance (text in them is not editable); export pages as PNG/JPG
  • OCR (searchable PDFs) in the languages your device supports, fully on-device
  • Sign PDFs: draw, type, use an image or your certificate (.p12/.pfx)
  • Password protection, watermarks, page numbers, metadata
  • Full PDF editor with text, images, drawings and shapes
  • Annotations: highlight, underline, strike through, notes and text

Free and Pro

  • You can open and try every tool for free and see the result before you decide.
  • 15 tools are free. Files you save or share from them carry a small “Edited by Editify” watermark.
  • Compress PDF, PDF to Word, PDF to Image, Text Recognition, Sign PDF and Edit PDF need Editify Pro to save or share their results. Pro also removes the watermark.
  • Ask the Document is free.

Private by design

  • Your documents are processed on your device and never sent to a server or an AI service.
  • The only tool that goes online is Web Page to PDF, to load the page you enter.
  • 100% native Apple frameworks — no third-party SDKs, no tracking
  • Available in 12 languages
  • Requires iOS 17.0 or later. Ask the Document needs iOS 26 or later on a device with Apple Intelligence turned on.
