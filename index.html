Editify

The offline PDF editor for iPhone and iPad

Editify is a privacy-first PDF editor built for iOS. Every operation — editing, signing, OCR, conversion — runs entirely on your device. Your documents never leave your iPhone.

What's inside

  • Merge, split, extract, rotate, crop, compress PDFs
  • Convert images, Word, RTF, web pages, camera scans → PDF
  • Convert PDF → Word (DOCX), image (PNG/JPG)
  • OCR (searchable PDFs) in 19+ languages, fully on-device
  • Sign PDFs (draw / type / image / X.509 certificate)
  • AES password protect, watermarks, page numbers, metadata
  • Full PDF editor with text, image, drawing, shapes
  • Annotations: highlight, underline, sticky notes, free text

Built with care

  • 100% native Apple frameworks — no third-party SDKs
  • Works fully offline; no servers, no tracking
  • Available in 12 languages
  • Requires iOS 16.0 or later
