Editify vs PDF Expert: an honest comparison

Updated November 2026 · written by Editify's developer

PDF Expert, by Readdle, is the more established and full-featured editor, with apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac under one Premium subscription. Editify is a smaller iPhone and iPad app that keeps every document on the device, needs no account and costs less. Choose PDF Expert if you work on a Mac too or want a long-proven app; choose Editify if privacy and price matter most.

Side by side

  • Devices: PDF Expert runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac. Editify runs on iPhone and iPad.
  • Price: PDF Expert Premium is listed at $79.99 a year for all your Apple devices, with a 7-day free trial. Editify Pro is $39.99 a year (with a 1-week free trial), $5.99 a month or $79.99 once for life. US App Store prices in November 2026; they vary by country.
  • Free use: both apps have a free version. In Editify, 15 tools are free with a small watermark on saved files, and every Pro tool shows its result before you pay.
  • AI: PDF Expert's PDF Copilot summarizes documents and answers questions with references. Editify's Ask the Document summarizes and answers questions with the source page, running entirely on the device with Apple Intelligence; it is free and needs iOS 26 on a supported device.
  • Privacy: Editify processes documents on your device only, has no account and no third-party SDKs; the only tool that goes online is Web Page to PDF.
  • Track record: PDF Expert has been on the App Store for years and has many ratings. Editify launched in 2026 and is still collecting its first ratings.

Questions

Which is better for privacy?

Editify keeps every document on your device and has no account. Check Readdle's privacy information for how PDF Expert handles documents, especially for cloud and AI features.

Which is cheaper?

In the US App Store in November 2026, Editify Pro costs $39.99 a year or $79.99 once; PDF Expert Premium costs $79.99 a year.

Can I use both?

Yes. Both open PDFs from the Files app, so you can try each with the same document.
