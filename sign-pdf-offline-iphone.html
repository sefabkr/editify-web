How to sign a PDF on iPhone without uploading it

Updated November 2026

Open the PDF in Editify, tap Sign PDF, draw or type your signature (or add it from an image), place it on the page and save. The whole job runs on your iPhone, so the document never leaves it. No account is needed.

Step by step

  1. Open Editify and pick the PDF from your recent documents, the Files app, Mail or any app that shares PDFs.
  2. Tap Sign PDF.
  3. Draw your signature, type it, or add a photo of it.
  4. Drag the signature to the right place and adjust its size.
  5. Look at the result, then save it to Editify, the Files app or share it.

Good to know

  • iOS also has a free Markup tool in the Files app that can add a quick drawn signature. Editify adds typed and image signatures, certificate signing (.p12/.pfx) and keeps all your PDF tools in one place.
  • Sign PDF is an Editify Pro tool: you can try it and see the signed result for free; saving or sharing it needs Editify Pro.

Questions

Is the PDF uploaded anywhere when I sign it?

No. Editify signs the PDF on your iPhone or iPad. Your documents are not sent to a server or an AI service.

Is a drawn signature legally valid?

A drawn or typed signature is an electronic signature. Whether it is accepted depends on who asks for it and where you are, so check with them. For a certificate-based digital signature, Editify can sign with your own .p12/.pfx certificate.

Can I sign without the internet?

Yes. Signing works offline because nothing is uploaded.
