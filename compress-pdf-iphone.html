How to compress a PDF on iPhone

Updated November 2026

Open the PDF in Editify, tap Compress PDF, choose one of three quality levels, check the size before and after, then save or share the smaller file. Compression runs on your iPhone, so the document is not uploaded to a website.

Step by step

  1. Open Editify and pick the PDF. The Home tab already shows an estimate of the compressed size.
  2. Tap Compress PDF and choose a quality level.
  3. Compare the size before and after on the result screen.
  4. Save the smaller copy or share it straight to Mail or a messaging app.

Good to know

  • Scanned pages and photos shrink the most. A PDF that is mostly text is already small and may barely change.
  • Compress PDF is an Editify Pro tool: you can see the compressed result and its size for free; saving or sharing it needs Editify Pro.

Questions

Does compressing lower the quality?

Images in the PDF are re-saved at the level you choose, so the strongest setting can make photos softer. Text stays sharp. Check the result before you save it.

Is my PDF uploaded to a website?

No. Editify compresses the file on your iPhone or iPad, with no upload and no account.

Why did my PDF hardly get smaller?

Compression mostly shrinks images. A PDF that is mostly text has little to remove.
